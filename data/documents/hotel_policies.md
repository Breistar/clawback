# Hotel Casa Alaria — Internal Policies

**Citation prefix:** `POL`
~90 rooms · historic center · own restaurant ("Alaria Cocina") and tour agency ("Alaria Tours").

## POL-01 — Cancellation policy

- **FLEX rates:** free cancellation until 48 hours before check-in; later cancellations incur a 1-night penalty.
- **NR rates:** non-refundable; full prepayment is retained on cancellation or no-show.

## POL-02 — Rate plans

| Plan | Early departure | Prepayment |
|---|---|---|
| **FLEX** | Unused nights are **refunded** at check-out | Card guarantee, charged at the stay |
| **NR** | No refund — hotel **retains the full booked amount** | 100% prepaid at booking |

> Commission implication: commission follows the money the hotel retained. FLEX early departure → refund issued → commission only on the nights charged. NR early departure → full amount retained → full commission is correct.

## POL-03 — No-show handling

Front desk registers no-shows in the PMS at the 23:00 night audit. The revenue team must mark each no-show on the corresponding OTA portal within that OTA's contractual window (Booking 48h per BKG-§5.1 · Expedia 72h per EXP-§4.1).

- FLEX no-show: 1-night fee may be charged; remainder released.
- NR no-show: full prepayment retained.

## POL-04 — Upgrades

Upgrades are offered only when the target room type has availability for the whole stay. Suites are excluded from proactive upgrade offers in December (high season).

## POL-05 — Direct booking channel

Direct rate = OTA public rate minus 8%, bookable via web, WhatsApp and phone. Direct guests receive a welcome drink.
