# Expedia Group — Lodging Partner Agreement

**Property:** Hotel Casa Alaria · **Hotel ID:** EXP-55107 · **Effective:** 2025-03-01
**Citation prefix:** `EXP`

## §1 — Scope

This Agreement governs distribution of Hotel Casa Alaria ("the Property") through Expedia Group points of sale and the compensation payable on bookings originated there.

## §2 — Compensation

**§2.1** The Property pays Expedia a commission of **15%** of the Booking Amount of every consumed booking.

**§2.2** "Booking Amount" means the room revenue actually charged to the guest and retained by the Property, excluding remitted taxes and incidental charges.

## §3 — Statements

**§3.1** A monthly statement is issued within the first 5 business days of the following month.

**§3.2** Statements are payable within 21 days.

## §4 — Status changes and no-shows

**§4.1** No-shows, cancellations and stay modifications must be reported through Expedia Partner Central **within 72 hours** of the scheduled check-in (no-shows) or of the triggering event (modifications).

**§4.2** Bookings reported within the window are re-rated to the amount actually retained by the Property.

## §5 — Statement disputes

**§5.1** Statement lines may be disputed **within 14 days** of statement issue, attaching folio and property-system evidence.

**§5.2** Undisputed lines are final.

## §6 — Cancellations

**§6.1** Cancellations inside the guest's free-cancellation window accrue no commission.

**§6.2** Where the Property waives its cancellation penalty as goodwill, no commission is due.

**§6.3** Where a guest cancels after the free-cancellation deadline and the Property **charges and retains a late-cancellation penalty** under its published rate policy, commission at the §2.1 rate is due on the penalty retained. Such charges are contractually valid and not disputable.

## §7 — Parity

Rate, inventory and content parity per Expedia Partner Central policies.
