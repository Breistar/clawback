# Hotel Casa Alaria — Win-Back Benefit Ladder

**Citation prefix:** `LAD`
Rules the agent must follow when building direct-booking offers per RFM segment. **Never discount a Champion.**

## LAD-01 — Segments (18-month window)

| Segment | Definition |
|---|---|
| **CHAMPION** | 4+ stays, last stay ≤ 60 days ago, high spend |
| **LOYAL** | 3+ stays, active within the last 6 months |
| **PROMISING** | 2 stays, first seen within the last 6 months |
| **DORMANT** | High historical spend, no stay in 6+ months |

## LAD-02 — Offer rules

| Segment | Direct benefit | Cross-sell | Post-stay |
|---|---|---|---|
| CHAMPION | Upgrade **subject to availability (check first — POL-04)** + late checkout. **NO discount.** | Preferred Alaria Tours pricing + priority table at Alaria Cocina | Thank-you + Google review invitation |
| LOYAL | Direct rate (POL-05) + welcome drink | Tour or restaurant perk matched to the guest's history | Review invite + next-visit offer |
| PROMISING | Sweetened direct rate (extra 5% off first direct stay) | Welcome courtesy | Soft follow-up at 30 days |
| DORMANT | Reactivation offer with a hook (e.g., free night on 3+ direct nights) | — | — |

## LAD-03 — Message rules

- One personal message per guest, in English for the demo.
- State the concrete benefit; never mention commissions or OTAs to the guest.
- Cross-sell must match evidence in the guest's history (restaurant spend → restaurant perk; tours → tour perk).
- The manager approves every message before sending.
