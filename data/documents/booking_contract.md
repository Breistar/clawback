# Booking.com — Accommodation Distribution Agreement

**Property:** Hotel Casa Alaria · **Property ID:** BKG-88412 · **Effective:** 2025-01-01
**Citation prefix:** `BKG`

## §1 — Scope

This Agreement governs the listing of Hotel Casa Alaria ("the Accommodation") on the Booking.com platform and the commissions payable on reservations originated through it.

## §2 — Commission

**§2.1** The Accommodation pays Booking.com a commission of **17%** of the Total Reservation Value of every Materialised Reservation.

**§2.2** "Total Reservation Value" means the amount actually charged to and retained from the guest for the stay, including mandatory fees, excluding the items listed in §7.

## §3 — Invoicing

**§3.1** A consolidated invoice is issued in the first week of each month covering the previous month.

**§3.2** Invoices are payable within 14 days of issue.

## §4 — Modifications and shortened stays

**§4.1** The Accommodation must register reservation modifications (date changes, shortened stays) through the Extranet.

**§4.2** Where a guest departs before the booked check-out date **and the Accommodation refunds the unused nights** under its rate policy, commission is due **only on the nights actually charged and retained**. The shortened stay must be registered in the Extranet within 48 hours of the actual check-out. Where the rate is non-refundable and the Accommodation retains the full payment, commission remains due on the full amount retained.

## §5 — No-shows

**§5.1** No-shows must be marked in the Extranet **within 48 hours** of the scheduled check-in. Reservations marked within this window accrue no commission, unless a no-show fee is charged to the guest, in which case commission applies to the fee retained.

**§5.2** No-shows not marked within the window are invoiced as Materialised Reservations.

## §6 — Invoice disputes

**§6.1** Invoice lines may be disputed **within 7 days** of the invoice issue date, with supporting evidence (PMS records, folio, Extranet log).

**§6.2** Lines not disputed within the window are deemed accepted.

## §7 — Non-commissionable charges

Excluded from the Total Reservation Value: taxes collected on behalf of authorities, resort fees, parking, restaurant and bar consumption, tours and third-party activities, damage deposits.

## §8 — Term

Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice. Accrued commissions remain payable.
